Frequently asked questions

What counts as "one post"?

Every photo you send that comes back treated, with a caption and hashtags, counts as one post — no matter how many times you accidentally resend the same photo.

Does the founder price go up later?

No. As long as your subscription stays active, it remains $2.90/month, even when the standard plan costs more for people joining after you.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, no penalty and no lock-in. Just let us know on WhatsApp.

What if I go over 50 posts in a month?

For now, just reach out and we'll sort it out by hand. Extra credit packs and bigger plans (Professional, Business) are on the way.
